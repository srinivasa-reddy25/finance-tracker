Before you install

Does it read my SMS or ask for my bank login?

No. Paisa asks for two things: internet and notifications. You log each spend yourself. It takes a few seconds, and it's the reason you'll actually remember where the money went.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Paisa is on Android.

Only rupees?

Only rupees, written the Indian way: ₹1,00,000, not ₹100,000.

Can I make my own categories?

Yes. Give it a name, an icon, a colour and, if you want, a monthly budget. "Others" always stays, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Can I get my data out?

Any time. CSV or PDF, for this month, last month, the last three months or everything, sent to your email.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes. Profile, then Dark appearance.
